- (decision by user) Use the BMAD coaching path with a Journey-led PRD; discussion is in Norwegian and the PRD artifact is in English.
- (decision by user) Use the generic physiotherapy student persona; do not invent a personal name.
- (decision by user) The student may generate multiple resource types from the same uploaded PDF within one study session.
- (decision by user) Generated resources are available only during the current session and are not saved for later.
- (decision by user) Flashcards show a question on the front; flipping reveals the answer, source page, and short excerpt. If page and excerpt are not returned, mark the item unverified.
- (decision by user) Quiz items use multiple choice; the student submits a choice before seeing the answer and source feedback.
- (assumption by coach) The app should process all extractable text from a supported PDF without silently omitting content; the practical file-size or page limit is still undecided.
- (note by user) The product brief uses two or three sample PDFs for evaluation; this is a test-set size, not a two-or-three-page upload limit.
- (note by coach) Official competitor scan found source-grounded citation features and flashcard/quiz generation in Gemini Notebook; frame differentiation as a focused workflow rather than unique citation capability.
- (decision by user) Confirmed the Journey-led study flow: upload one PDF, generate multiple resource types from it within the current session, use flip-to-reveal flashcards, and submit a multiple-choice quiz answer before viewing answer/source feedback.
- (decision by user) Supported PDFs are text-based and limited to 50 pages and 20 MB; the selected provider must be checked against those limits during architecture, and the limits must be revised before implementation if the provider cannot support them.
- (decision by user) Source references use the PDF viewer page numbers, not printed page numbers. All four resource types show page numbers when available; summaries show no excerpt, while flashcards, quiz questions, and key concepts show a short source excerpt.
- (decision by user) Each flashcard or quiz generation creates up to 10 items, fewer when the PDF does not support more; quiz questions have three options, whole-quiz submission, correctness coloring, brief source-grounded explanations, and a different question order on retake.
- (decision by user) Norwegian is the main language of generated resources; relevant Latin anatomical terms are retained, and an explicitly requested Latin term is answered in Latin with surrounding text in Norwegian.
- (decision by user) PDFs and generated resources are session-scoped in the app; the selected language-model provider's processing and retention practices must be reviewed during architecture.
